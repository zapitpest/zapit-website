# Client Communication Template — Adam / Zap It

Adam's prescribed structure for all substantive project updates. Locked in on 23 July 2026, refined on 27 July 2026 with 5 additional sections.

Use this template verbatim for every substantive update. Short internal-decision confirmations and one-line replies do not need it.

---

## Template structure

### 1. Roadmap (added 27 Jul per Adam feedback #1 and #2)

Simple visual overview of every phase. Adam's exact eight-phase list. Percentages are per-phase, not overall MVP.

**MVP scope (required before launch):**
- ✅ Phase 1 – Website
- ✅ Phase 2 – Analytics & Tracking
- 🟡 Phase 3 – CEO Dashboard (X%)
- ⬜ Phase 4 – Hosting & DNS
- ⬜ Phase 5 – Website Launch

**Phase 2 scope (next priorities after MVP):**
- ⬜ Phase 6 – CRM Integration

**Future roadmap:**
- ⬜ Phase 7 – AI Optimisation
- ⬜ Phase 8 – Ongoing Improvements

Markers: ✅ done, 🟡 in progress with %, ⬜ not started.

### 2. Overall status

- MVP status: approximately X% complete
- One sentence on where the project stands

### 3. Completed

Split into three buckets, do not merge.

**Live and working**

**Complete but waiting for activation**

**Designed or planned, but not yet built**

### 4. Currently being worked on

Short ordered list of tasks Apex is actively completing now.

### 5. What you can continue without me

Everything Apex will progress independently while Adam is unblocking other items.

### 6. What you need from me

Per item, always include ALL six fields:
- Action: exactly what Adam needs to do
- Where: which tool, URL, or account
- What is needed: the exact access, value, or decision
- Time: how long it should take him
- Blocked until done: what stays blocked
- Can Apex do it with access: yes with access X, or no

### 7. Decisions required

Per decision:
- Recommended option
- Why
- Cost or risk difference
- Delay impact

Adam has said explicitly: give him a recommendation, not a menu.

### 8. Current Risks (added 27 Jul per Adam feedback #3)

Either the word "None." or a short bullet list of live risks. Every risk names what specifically it blocks.

Examples:
- Waiting on DNS TXT verification before Search Console can be activated
- Netlify credit burning while hosting decision is pending
- None

Never omit this section, even when it reads "None." — Adam wants confidence nothing is missed.

### 9. Project hours (added 27 Jul per Adam feedback #4)

- Hours this period: X hr
- Total hours to date: Y hr
- Estimated hours remaining for MVP: Z hr (approximate)

Precise numbers, honest under-billing pattern preserved.

### 10. Next milestone

- What Adam will see next
- When Apex expects to send it
- What happens after his review

### 11. What This Means For Adam

Short plain-English business summary. No jargon.

### 12. Top Priorities (added 27 Jul per Adam feedback #5)

Short ordered list of the current focus. Example format:
1. Complete Dashboard Pages 2 to 6
2. Hosting migration
3. DNS cutover
4. Website launch

Adam uses this to see team focus at a glance.

---

## Tone rules

- Plain English throughout. No AI-tell words (seamless, robust, cutting-edge, leverage, delve, etc.).
- Sentences short.
- Numbers precise. Never round vaguely.
- Every "we will do X" has a time estimate.
- Every "we need Y from you" has all 6 fields from Section 6.
- No paragraphs longer than 3 lines.
- No confidence-inflating adjectives ("comprehensive", "extensive", "world-class"). Just what's true.
- Human tone, no em-dashes as bullet markers, no AI-formatting tells.
